Find automation parts and instruments, build one request and receive an IES quotation within one working day. No account and no online payment are needed.

01What the store is

The IES Store is a quotation store (Phase 1). You choose items and send a request for quotation (RFQ). IES engineers check availability, condition and lead time and send you a written quotation with prices in USD or IQD.

  • Payment after you accept the quotation: Qi Card transfer, bank transfer or purchase order (PO).
  • Delivery across Iraq from our Baghdad warehouse, or imported to order.
  • Reply time: one working day (Saturday–Thursday, 08:00–16:00 Baghdad time).

02Find a part

  1. Type a part number, model or description in the search box at the top (for example 6ES7, radar, Triconex). Spaces and dashes do not matter.
  2. Or press one of the quick chips under the search box.
  3. Choose the division: Industrial (control systems, instruments, legacy spares) or Building (BMS, KNX, fire alarm, instruments).
  4. Choose a section tab. The number on each tab shows how many items it holds.

Nothing found? Press Request “…” under the empty result: your search text is added to the RFQ and we source it for you.

03Filters, sorting and views

  • Availability – In stock (Baghdad), 4–6 weeks delivery (Supmea) or Sourced on request (legacy spares).
  • Product type, Brand and Condition narrow the list; the small number shows how many items match.
  • Clear filters removes all filters. On a phone, open the filters with the Filters button.
  • Sort by best match, part number or most available, and switch between grid and list view.

05Add items to your RFQ

  1. Set the quantity with − and + on the card.
  2. Press the green + button. It turns into ✓ and the RFQ counter at the side of the screen goes up.
  3. Click the part number or the picture to see details: manufacturer, country of origin, HS code, lifecycle and successor.
↑ Back to top

06Configure a Supmea instrument

  1. Open Instruments and press Configure & add on a Supmea model.
  2. Choose what you know: size or range, output signal, process connection, quantity. Add notes such as medium, temperature or number of tags.
  3. Press Add to RFQ. IES engineers confirm the final order code and send the datasheet with the quotation.

The Datasheet (PDF) link opens the manufacturer datasheet.

↑ Back to top

07Paste your part list (legacy spares)

  1. Open Industrial › Legacy Spares.
  2. Paste your list in the box: one part number per line, with the quantity after it if you know it (for example 3805E 2).
  3. Choose the brand if all parts are from one brand (optional) and press Check & add to RFQ.

Each line is checked: items found in IES stock are matched, listed spares are added, and the rest are added for sourcing. All lines go into one RFQ.

↑ Back to top

08Send your RFQ

  1. Press Request a quote (or the RFQ button at the side). Your RFQ opens with all lines.
  2. Check quantities, remove lines you do not need and add a note to any line.
  3. Fill in Company, Contact name and Phone / WhatsApp (required), and if you can: e-mail, delivery city, project or PO reference, required date and a message.
  4. Press Send RFQ. You see your RFQ number (for example RFQ-261127-AB12). If you gave an e-mail address, you also receive a confirmation e-mail.

You can also send the same RFQ by e-mail or WhatsApp, or download it as a CSV file for your own records.

09After you send

  • An IES engineer reviews the RFQ and replies by e-mail or WhatsApp within one working day, usually with the quotation.
  • Quote your RFQ number when you call or write to us.
  • Your RFQ is kept in the browser until you send it or empty it, so you can add items over several visits.

10Your data and product names

  • We use your contact details only to answer your request and to prepare the quotation. We do not sell or share them.
  • Brand names and part numbers identify products only. Legacy spares are supplied from independent stock, not as manufacturer-authorised supply.
