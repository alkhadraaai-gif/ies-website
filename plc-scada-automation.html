04

Frequently asked questions

Which control systems does IES work with?

Mainly Siemens: SIMATIC S7-1500, S7-1200 and S7-400 PLCs, PCS 7 DCS, ET 200 remote I/O, WinCC SCADA and HMI, SINAMICS drives and SIRIUS motor controls, engineered in TIA Portal and PCS 7.

Can you upgrade an old control system without stopping the plant?

Yes. We survey the existing system, plan a step-by-step migration and schedule cut-overs around plant operation, as we did on the Baiji power station burner management retrofit.

Do you build control panels and MCCs?

Yes. We design and build PLC cabinets, unit control panels and motor control centres to project specifications, and test them before delivery to site.

Which industries do you serve?

Power generation, oil & gas, refineries, cement, water and wastewater, and utilities, across Iraq from our Baghdad and Basra offices.

Have an I/O list, a control philosophy or a tender? Send it and our engineers will scope it.
